Colombo Court / Events

Nobody else’s idea
of an evening.

Six spaces in one small hotel in Colombo 3, each of which becomes whatever the night is for.

Why you should choose us.

Because the chef will cook your grandmother’s curry. Because there is no set banqueting card here, no printed list of three packages to choose between, and no head office to write to for permission. Tell us who is coming, what they eat and what the evening is actually for, and a course list comes back before you agree to anything. Bring your own decorator, your own band, your own photographer. We take no commission and keep no approved supplier list, and the answer to almost everything is yes.

And because none of this was built to hold functions. The hotel was two office buildings with a garden cut into the middle of them, so there is no ballroom, no carpet and no shape your evening has to fit inside. Thirty-one rooms means the person who quotes you is the person standing here on the night, and the kitchen that sends out your dinner is the same one that made breakfast. In Colombo that is genuinely unusual, and it is the whole reason people find us.

  • 150 standing, at the most
  • 31 rooms, yours if you want them
  • 1 person, first call to last guest

Three different rooms.

A board meeting, a proposal and a wedding, with nothing brought in but what the guests brought.

  1. A board meeting

    The Scarlet Room. One long table, a projector, and lunch out of the same kitchen that cooks the weddings.

  2. A proposal

    Cloud, the rooftop. One table, the city underneath it, and everybody else quietly kept off that corner.

  3. A wedding

    The courtyard. Drapes across the whole garden, pale florals, gold chairs, and the pool lit underneath it.

The same three rooms also take engagements, hen nights and bridal showers, christenings, farewells, birthdays at both ends of life, product launches, training days, and the annual dinner of a company that has had enough of hotel ballrooms. One courtyard has been a wedding on Saturday and a sixth birthday with a balloon arch and comic panels up the wall on Sunday.

How many guests?

The first question we ask, so it is the first one the page answers. Real numbers, not optimistic ones.

  1. Up to 25

    Four rooms take this

    Eight around one table in the library, twenty-five as a boardroom in the Scarlet Room, or drinks in the Loft that do not want a whole courtyard around them. Grape Expectations takes a tasting.

  2. 25 to 50

    The size we were built for

    Forty seated at the water’s edge, sixty on the roof, forty-five in the Loft. At this number you can take two spaces and move between them, which is what most parties end up doing anyway.

  3. 50 to 100

    The courtyard or the roof

    Cloud takes ninety at a banquet and a hundred and fifty standing; the courtyard takes a hundred and twenty seated. Under a hundred the choice is really garden or skyline.

  4. 100 to 150

    The courtyard, and the roof too

    A hundred and twenty seated in the garden or a hundred and fifty standing, with Cloud open above for drinks before and dancing after. This is the largest we go, and we say so now.

The six spaces.

Two outdoors, four in. None of them was built to be a function room, which is why none of them feels like one.

The courtyard

The one people book the hotel for.

Sixteen hundred square feet of planted garden with the lap pool down the middle of it and mature trees over the top. It is the largest space here and the only one that is genuinely outdoors, and it is where the drapes, the arches and the balloon walls go. Amber Poolside cooks into it, so the kitchen is thirty feet from the table.

  • 150 standing
  • 120 banquet
  • 40 seated at one table
  • 1,600 sq ft

Cloud, the rooftop

Two and a half thousand square feet with the city underneath it.

The terrace came back this year from a full refurbishment, with a glass roof drawn over part of it, so the good seats stay good whatever the sky is doing. A proper bar, a semi fine dining kitchen and the lights of Colombo 3 beyond the rail. It is the biggest space by area and the one that suits a standing evening best.

  • 150 standing
  • 90 banquet
  • 60 seated
  • 2,500 sq ft

The Scarlet Room

The only room here that was built for work.

One long timber table, deep red walls, a projector and a screen, and daylight from the garden side rather than the strip light and no windows most meeting rooms give you. Board meetings, interviews, client pitches and training days, and a private dining room in the evening once the screen goes up.

  • 30 theatre
  • 25 boardroom
  • 25 classroom
  • 625 sq ft

The Loft

An indoor bar and lounge with a lit counter and low seating down one side. Dark, warm and enclosed, which is what you want for a hen night, a launch, or the part of an evening that starts once dinner has finished.

  • 45 seated
  • 30 for drinks
  • 775 sq ft

Grape Expectations

The wine lounge. Deep white sofas, low tables and a considered list, open from eleven in the morning until eleven at night. The room for a tasting, a small engagement, or an anniversary that wants somewhere quiet with a door on it.

  • Sofas not rows
  • 11:00 to 23:00
  • Wine on request

The library

Floor to ceiling shelves, leather chairs and one table for eight. The smallest room we have, and the one asked for most by people who want a meeting nobody walks past. There is no projector, which is usually the reason.

  • 8 at one table
  • 140 sq ft
  • No projector

Menu

The events menu

Buffets, set menus, high tea and canapés, written out so you can see what a course actually is rather than what it is called. There is something on it for every kind of evening, and the chef will write something that is not on it for yours.

Package

The conference package

The Scarlet Room and the library as a long table, a classroom or a theatre, with the projector, the screen and the whiteboard included rather than added at the end. Tell us how the day runs and the breaks and the lunch move to fit it.

Neither is fixed, and most events change something on them. We do buffets, plated dinners, high tea, canapés passed around, a grill by the pool, or one long table with a menu written for the people sitting at it. If what you want is on neither document, that is usually the conversation we most enjoy having.

These are the nights themselves, filmed as they happened rather than staged for a brochure. There are more of them, and newer ones, on Instagram and TikTok.

What we will change for you.

Almost all of it. That is not a slogan, it is what a thirty-one room hotel can do that a two hundred room hotel cannot, because the person you ask is the person who decides.

The menu is the usual place people expect a no and get a yes. There is no set banqueting card here. Tell the chef who is coming, what they eat, what they will not eat and what the evening is for, and a course list comes back to you before you agree to anything. Family recipes, a grandmother’s curry, a dish from a restaurant you liked in another country: bring it and we will cook it.

Beyond the food, the honest answer is that we start from yes and work backwards. Your own decorator, your own band, your own photographer, furniture brought in, the pool covered or lit or left alone, the whole hotel emptied of other guests. The things we say no to are the things that would spoil somebody else’s night, and there are not many of those.

The only thing we will not do is pretend. If a hundred and eighty people will not fit, we will say so on the phone rather than at the tasting.

Before you enquire.

The questions the events team answers most, in case one of them is yours.

How many people can you actually hold?

A hundred and fifty standing, in the courtyard or on the rooftop. A hundred and twenty seated at a banquet in the courtyard, ninety on the roof. Those are the real numbers rather than the hopeful ones, and above a hundred and fifty we will tell you to look elsewhere.

Can we bring our own decorator, band or photographer?

Yes to all three, and most people do. Tell us who they are and when they need access, and we will let them in early to set up. We have no approved supplier list and take no commission for using ours instead.

Can the chef cook something that is not on any menu?

That is the normal way of doing it here. There is no set banqueting card. Tell us who is coming, what they eat and what the evening is for, and a course list comes back before you agree to anything. A family recipe or a dish from somewhere else is welcome; bring it and we will cook it.

Can we take the whole hotel?

Yes. All thirty-one rooms can be booked at once, which means no other guests in the lift, in the courtyard or at breakfast. It needs the most notice of anything we do, so ask about it first rather than last.

What happens if it rains?

Colombo rain arrives in bursts of under an hour rather than all day. Part of the rooftop is under a glass roof, the courtyard is shaded by its own planting, and the Loft, the Scarlet Room and Grape Expectations are all indoors and all a few steps away. We agree the wet-weather plan when you book, and we tell you who decides and by what hour.

Do you do corporate meetings as well as parties?

Yes, and they are a good deal of what we do. The Scarlet Room is a proper meeting room with a projector, seating twenty-five as a boardroom or thirty in theatre rows. The library takes eight around one table for anything that needs a door and no screen. Lunch comes from the same kitchen as everything else, which is the part people remember.

Is there parking, and how do guests find you?

Parking is free and on site at 32 Alfred House Avenue, in Colombo 3. We are a few minutes from Independence Square and Galle Face Green, which is worth putting on the invitation because most guests know both.

How far ahead should we book?

For a wedding or exclusive use of the hotel, several months. For a dinner, a meeting or a birthday, a few weeks is usually enough and a few days is sometimes possible. Send us the date whatever it is and we will tell you straight away rather than keep you waiting.

Can you help with a proposal?

We have done a good many. The usual shape is dinner for two in the courtyard with the pool lit and everybody else kept away from it, and the rest is whatever you have planned. Tell us the plan on WhatsApp rather than by email, because the timing is the part that matters and it is easier to get right in a conversation.

Do you have a minimum spend?

For exclusive use of a space on a Friday or Saturday evening, yes, and it depends on the space and the season. We will tell you the figure in the first reply rather than after three meetings.

Tell us what the night is for.

We will tell you honestly whether we are the right place for it.
