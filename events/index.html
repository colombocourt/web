Our five venues.

Two outdoor and three indoor venues, each with its own character.

The poolside garden

Our largest venue, and the favourite for weddings.

A 1,600 sq ft planted garden with the lap pool at its centre and mature trees overhead. It is the setting for weddings, receptions and large celebrations, with plenty of room for drapes, floral arches and themed décor. Food is served fresh from the Amber Poolside kitchen, just a few steps away.

  • 120 banquet
  • 1,600 sq ft

Cloud rooftop

Rooftop terrace with views across Colombo.

Recently refurbished, the terrace has a glass roof over part of the space. With a full bar, its own kitchen and the city lights all around, Cloud is ideal for cocktail evenings, receptions and parties.

  • 90 banquet
  • 60 seated
  • 2,500 sq ft

The Scarlet Room

Our conference room, and a private dining room by night.

One long timber table, deep red walls, a projector and screen, and natural daylight. The room suits conferences, meetings, interviews, presentations and training days, and becomes a private dining room in the evening.

  • 650 sq ft

The Loft

An indoor bar and lounge with a lit counter and comfortable low seating. Its warm, intimate setting suits hen parties, product launches and drinks after dinner.

The library

Floor-to-ceiling bookshelves, leather armchairs and one table for eight. Our most private meeting room, ideal for confidential meetings and small dinners.

Frequently asked questions.

Answers to the questions our events team is asked most often.

How many guests can you host?

The poolside garden takes up to 120 guests for a seated banquet and the Cloud rooftop up to 90. The Scarlet Room seats up to 25, the Loft hosts up to 45 and the library seats up to 8.

Can we bring our own decorator, band or photographer?

Yes. You are welcome to work with your own suppliers. Please share their details and arrival times with us at least five days before the event so we can arrange access for set-up. Decorations must be free-standing and approved by the hotel in advance.

Can the menu be customised?

Yes. We have our own events menus and we are always happy to customise them. You can choose from the menu or ask our chef to create a menu for your event. Tell us about your guests, any dietary needs and the style of service you would like, and we will send you a menu proposal. Final menus are confirmed seven days before the event.

Can we book the whole hotel?

Yes. All 31 rooms can be reserved for your group, so the hotel is exclusively yours for the occasion. Exclusive use needs the most notice, so please mention it early in your enquiry.

Can you arrange rooms for our guests?

Yes. The hotel has 31 rooms and suites, and we can reserve rooms for your guests alongside the event. Tell us how many rooms you need in the enquiry form and we will include them in your quotation.

Do you host corporate meetings?

Yes. The Scarlet Room is a dedicated meeting room with a projector and screen. It seats 25 theatre style, 20 classroom style, or 15 around a conference table. The library is ideal for private meetings of up to 8 people. Refreshments and meals are prepared by our own kitchen.

How late can an event run?

In line with Sri Lankan noise regulations, music must finish by 10:30pm. Last orders for food and drinks are also at 10:30pm.

Is there parking, and where are you?

Yes, parking is free on site. We are at 32 Alfred House Avenue, Colombo 3, a few minutes from Independence Square and Galle Face Green.

How far ahead should we book?

For weddings and exclusive use of the hotel, we recommend booking several months ahead. For dinners, meetings and birthdays, a few weeks is usually enough. Send us your date and we will confirm availability as soon as possible.

Can you help plan a proposal?

Yes, we arrange proposals regularly. A popular choice is a private dinner for two on the rooftop or beside the pool, styled around your plans. Send us an enquiry so we can agree the details and timing with you.

Is there a minimum spend?

This depends on the occasion, the venue and the date. Share your plans with us and we will send you a clear quotation.
