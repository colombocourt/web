About this notice

This website is owned and controlled by Colombo Court Hotel (Pvt) Ltd under the brand name Colombo Court Hotel & Spa (CCH).

This Privacy Notice explains who we are, how we collect, share and use Personal Information, and how you can exercise your privacy rights. It includes information about:

  • What information we collect
  • How we collect it
  • How we use it
  • The security measures we take concerning your Personal Information
  • Whether it is shared, and with whom
  • Your rights concerning your Personal Information
02

What does Colombo Court Hotel & Spa do?

Colombo Court Hotel & Spa operates a boutique hotel, The Court Spa, Cloud Café, the Loft Bar, the Scarlet Room restaurant and Amber Poolside Café in the city of Colombo, Sri Lanka.

This privacy policy contains information on how CCH collects, uses and shares information collected from you, and how that information is protected from unauthorised access. This privacy policy covers only the Site. By using the Site, you agree that CCH may collect, use and share information consistent with this privacy policy.

03

Effective date and changes to this policy

The effective date of this privacy policy is 30 May 2018.

CCH may change this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will become effective upon posting of the revised policy on the Site.

If any of the changes affect how CCH treats or handles personally identifiable information or personal data collected from you, such as your name, address, telephone number, email address, credit card number or any other information that could identify you (“Personal Information”), we will give notice of this on our website. You may inform us by email of any changes to the consent you have given us by using this website.

04

Collection and use of information

CCH will state explicitly on the website that Personal Information is requested only for the following purposes: when making reservations, when requesting a specific service, if you need us to contact you for any reason, to operate the Site, to provide Site services, to administer services reserved through the Site, to contact you in response to a question or about a good or service you purchased or reserved through the Site, and/or to inform you of new features, services and products.

CCH does not sell or otherwise make your Personal Information available to any third parties for their own use. However, subject to the applicable laws of Sri Lanka, CCH may collect, use and disclose relevant portions of your Personal Information to our business partners in order, among other things, to:

  • Process your online reservations and bookings for our hotel accommodation and spa services.
  • Provide, and charge for, the hotel accommodation, spa services and other goods and services you purchase.
  • Provide you with a better, more personalised level of service.
  • Fulfil contractual obligations to you, to anyone involved in making your travel arrangements (such as travel agents or group travel organisers), and to vendors (such as credit card companies and airline operators).
  • Conduct market research, customer satisfaction and quality assurance surveys, and direct marketing and sales promotions.

CCH may collect your name, email address and telephone number, as required by law in Sri Lanka, and any other information you choose to provide so that we may process your requests. All required and optional information is clearly marked on any form you are presented with. If you give us feedback or contact us by email, we will collect your name and email address, as well as any other content included in the email, in order to send you a reply.

The Personal Information that we may collect about you broadly falls into the following categories.

Information you provide voluntarily

CCH asks you to provide certain information voluntarily. This includes your contact details in order to make a reservation, to book or purchase one of our services, to subscribe to marketing communications from us, and to submit enquiries to us. CCH will also ask you for your credit card details, so that we can charge you for any purchases you make during your stay at the hotel. If we ask you to provide any other Personal Information not described above, this will be made clear to you at the point we collect it.

Information we collect automatically

We may also collect certain information automatically from your device, which may or may not fall under applicable personal information protection laws. This may include your IP address, device type, unique device identification numbers, browser type, broad geographic location (for example country or city-level location) and other technical information, as well as how your device has interacted with our website, including the pages accessed and links clicked. This helps us understand the visitors who come to our website, where they come from and what content interests them. We use it for internal analytics and to improve the quality and relevance of our website and of the services provided to guests at the hotel. Some of this information may be collected using cookies and similar tracking technology.

05

Cookies

Some information and data may be collected automatically from you through the standard operation of our internet servers and through the use of “cookies”. Cookies are small text files that a site can store on your computer. CCH uses cookies to improve your experience by recognising repeat users, making it easier to keep accessing and using the Site, tracking Site usage behaviour, and compiling aggregate data that allows content improvements and targeted advertising. Cookies generally neither damage your files nor allow us to identify you personally, and some cookies expire as soon as you leave our site. Others remain on your browser so that we can recognise you when you return. Cookies can be disabled at any time in your browser settings. However, some cookies may be necessary to provide some features of the Site, so deleting them may affect how the Site works for you.

Please note that by using the Site, you consent to the CCH website privacy policy.

The cookies this website uses, and how to accept or refuse them, are set out in full in our Cookie Policy.

CCH does not sell any of your Personal Information to third parties for any reason. As explained above, CCH may share some of your Personal Information with certain third parties in order to run its business properly, including the operation of its spa and hotel services. For example, your name and credit card information will be shared with our credit card processing vendor in order to process payments.

CCH may also disclose your Personal Information if required to do so by law, or in the good-faith belief that such action is necessary: (a) to comply with applicable law or with legal process served on CCH or the Site; (b) to protect and defend the rights or property of CCH or the Site; or (c) if the ownership of CCH changes, such as it being acquired by another party.

Retention of data

CCH keeps Personal Information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for, or for as long as the law requires. This includes our duties under the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 of Sri Lanka, and under Sri Lankan tax, accounting and company law.

When we have no ongoing legitimate business need to process your Personal Information, we will either delete or anonymise it or, if this is not possible (for example, because your Personal Information has been stored in backup archives), we will securely store it and isolate it from any further processing until deletion is possible.

We respond to all requests we receive from individuals wishing to exercise their data protection rights in accordance with applicable data protection laws. How we apply the General Data Protection Regulation is explained on our GDPR compliance page.

15

Customer advisory on phishing emails

At Colombo Court Hotel & Spa, the privacy and online safety of our guests are of the utmost importance. There has recently been a rise in phishing and fraudulent email activity worldwide, affecting many hotels and booking platforms.

These deceptive messages may impersonate legitimate hotel brands or online booking channels in order to steal personal or payment information. While this is a global issue affecting the hospitality industry as a whole, we want to make sure our guests stay informed and protected.

How to identify a phishing email

Please be cautious if you receive any message that:

  • Requests payment or credit card details by email or through an unfamiliar link.
  • Uses an unofficial or misspelt email address (for example, one not ending in @colombocourthotel.com).
  • Contains poor grammar, suspicious attachments, or urgent language urging you to act immediately.
  • Directs you to websites that do not begin with https://www.colombocourthotel.com or a verified booking platform.

Our communication policy

  • We never request credit card or payment details by email.
  • All genuine booking or payment communications will come from our official domain (@colombocourthotel.com) or through our trusted travel partners.
  • Our team may contact you only about confirmed reservations or official hotel updates.

What you should do

If you suspect a phishing email:

  • Do not click on any links or attachments.
  • Do not reply to the sender.
  • Forward the suspicious email to our official address, info@colombocourthotel.com, for verification.
  • Delete the message from your inbox immediately.

We are committed to safeguarding your personal information and to working closely with our partners to ensure a secure digital environment for all our guests.

Colombo Court Hotel & Spa will not be liable for any security breach or financial loss resulting from the disclosure of personal information through unofficial or fraudulent emails. Your vigilance plays a vital role in maintaining the integrity of our shared online environment.

For any concerns, please contact info@colombocourthotel.com, or call +94 11 464 5333.
