Before you book.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is at 12:00 noon. If you would like to arrive earlier or enjoy a little more time before you leave, early check-in and late check-out can be arranged subject to availability and applicable charges. Simply contact our team before your arrival and we will be happy to check what is possible.

How far is the airport, and can you arrange a transfer?

Bandaranaike International Airport is approximately 35 km from Colombo Court, with the journey usually taking around 45 minutes to 1 hour by expressway, depending on traffic. We can arrange a private airport transfer in an air-conditioned vehicle at any time of day or night. Simply share your flight details with us and our team will take care of the arrangements.

Can we have an extra bed, or rooms next to each other?

Extra beds can be arranged in Superior Deluxe rooms and above, subject to room configuration and availability. Extra beds are not available in Deluxe Rooms, the Luxury Duplex Suite or the Luxury Honeymoon Suite. If you are travelling together and would like rooms next to each other, we will be happy to arrange this where possible, subject to availability at the time of arrival. Let us know when you book.

Do the rooms have outdoor space?

Selected rooms across our categories offer a private outdoor space, with either a private courtyard or private balcony. These spaces are allocated subject to availability at the time of arrival. If having outdoor space is important to your stay, please let our team know when making your reservation and we will do our best to accommodate your preference.

What comes with the room?

Your stay includes more than just your room. Guests can enjoy complimentary Wi-Fi, access to the pool and fitness room, and time in our library and other hotel spaces. You can also enjoy breakfast and dining at Amber Poolside, evenings at Cloud Café & Bar, and wellness experiences at The Court Spa, making it easy to have a complete Colombo stay without having to leave the hotel.

Are the rooms non-smoking?

Yes. Colombo Court is a non-smoking hotel, and smoking is not permitted inside guest rooms or Suites. A USD 300 cleaning fee will apply if smoking takes place in a room. We appreciate your cooperation in keeping the hotel comfortable and welcoming for all guests.

Do you have accessible rooms?

We can accommodate guests with accessibility requirements, subject to availability and the specific room configuration. Our ground floor, garden and restaurant areas are step-free, and our team is happy to discuss your requirements before arrival. Please contact us when making your reservation so we can recommend the most suitable arrangements for your stay.

What is your cancellation and payment policy?

Our cancellation and payment conditions vary depending on the time of year. To keep things simple, here is how they work:

Off season (all other dates)
A 24-hour cancellation notice is required before arrival.
Cancellations made within the applicable period are refundable according to the booking terms.

Season (20 December to 31 March, and July and August)
A 14-day cancellation notice is required before arrival.
Cancellations made 14 days or more before arrival are refundable according to the booking terms.
Cancellations made within 14 days of arrival are non-refundable.

Payment
Booking confirmation requires a 50% advance payment.
The remaining balance is payable according to the applicable booking terms and season.

Your reservation confirmation will always state the specific cancellation and payment conditions applicable to your stay. If you have a question about your booking, ask our reception team.

Are children welcome at Colombo Court?

Yes. We welcome families and have room options that can provide additional space and comfort.

Infants to 5 years: stay free of charge, including meals.
6 to 12 years: 50% of the applicable meal rate, plus an extra bed charge of USD 15 per night.
Above 12 years: considered an adult and charged at the applicable adult rate.

Please let us know the ages of your children when making your reservation so we can recommend the most suitable room or Suite.

Which cards and currencies do you accept?

We accept American Express, Visa and Mastercard.

For your convenience, we also accept payments in:
Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR)
US Dollars (USD)
British Pounds (GBP)
Australian Dollars (AUD)
Euros (EUR)

Currency conversion is subject to the applicable exchange rate at the time of payment.

Can I bring my pet?

Unfortunately, we are not a pet-friendly hotel and are unable to accommodate pets on the property. If you have any questions about your stay or special requirements, please contact our team before booking.
