around one garden.

See the rooms Check availability

What separates one from the next.

Colombo Court was built inward, around a planted court with a lap pool running its length. So the rooms are not ranked by how high up they are or how far they can see. They are ranked by how much floor they have, and how much room they leave you to be quiet in.

The suites sit at the top of that, and there are more of them than of anything else: fifteen of the thirty-one. Below them the Superior Deluxe, the Deluxe Twin and the Deluxe Room are smaller and simpler, and they open onto the same trees. Nothing here faces a car park.

Suite

The room we have most of.

Four hundred and seventy-five square feet, a four-poster king under a ceiling fan, a proper seating corner and a writing desk with a lamp over it. The bathroom is separate and has a bath as well as the rain shower.

A suite here costs about what a room costs elsewhere, which is the happy consequence of the building having more suites in it than anything else. Selected ground-floor suites open onto a walled courtyard of their own, and certain suites can be arranged next to each other.

A Nescafé Dolce Gusto machine, an iron and board already in the room, and an extra bed on request. Selected suites take four guests rather than three, so say how many of you there are when you book.

Before you book.

The questions reservations answers most, in case one of them is yours.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is at 12:00 noon. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and chargeable. Send us your flight times when you book and we will arrange it if we can.

How far is the airport, and can you arrange a transfer?

Bandaranaike International is about 35 km away, which is 45 to 60 minutes depending on the traffic. Airport transfers are arranged on request, so send us the flight number and someone will be waiting with your name.

Which room should I book if I want the most space?

The Duplex Suite, at 704 sq ft over two levels. After that the Suites and the Honeymoon Suite at 475 sq ft each, then Superior Deluxe at 320, then the Deluxe Twin and the Deluxe Room at 275.

Can we have an extra bed, or rooms next to each other?

An extra bed goes into a Suite, the Honeymoon Suite, the Duplex Suite or a Superior Deluxe on request. Certain rooms and suites can be arranged next to one another, subject to availability. Ask when you book rather than at the desk.

Which rooms have a bath rather than only a shower?

Every Suite, the Honeymoon Suite and the Duplex Suite have a bath, and so do selected Superior Deluxe rooms. Every room in the hotel has an indoor rain shower.

Is breakfast included?

It depends on the rate. Meal plans are flexible: room only, room and breakfast, or half board with dinner as well. Tell us which suits the trip and we will quote it, rather than bundle in a meal you will not be here to eat.

Do the rooms have outdoor space?

Selected rooms have a balcony or a terrace, and selected ground-floor suites open onto a walled courtyard of their own, both subject to availability. Ask for one when you book, because they are the rooms that go first.

What comes with the room?

Wi-Fi throughout the hotel, the lap pool, the fitness room, the library and free parking on site. Both restaurants and The Court Spa are open to you, and laundry, dry cleaning and airport transfers are arranged on request.

Are the rooms non-smoking?

All thirty-one of them, and that is not negotiable, because the building is small and the courtyard carries everything. There is plenty of outdoor space to smoke in, and nobody will make you feel awkward about using it.

Is it suitable for working from?

Every room has a reading table and Wi-Fi that reaches all of it. The Duplex Suite has an iMac and a living room on a separate floor from the bed, which is the one to take for a long stay. The library and the Scarlet Room are there for anything that needs a door.

What is nearby?

Independence Square and its Arcade, the Gangaramaya Temple, Colombo City Centre and Galle Face Green are all a few minutes away, and the Fort is a short drive. We are at 32 Alfred House Avenue, in Colombo 3.
