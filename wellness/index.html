What actually happens.

Most people are a little unsure the first time. Here is the whole thing, in order, so you are not.

  1. You arrive

    Your therapist meets you before anything else. Trained in Bali, and asking first where you carry the tension, what you have injured, how much pressure you want and what to leave alone. Two minutes, and it shapes the whole hour.

  2. What suits you

    Balinese, deep tissue, aromatherapy, an Ayurvedic treatment, or a facial made with island botanicals. We will tell you which one answers what you have just described, and how long it needs, not which one costs most.

  3. The treatment

    It opens with a foot ritual: warm water, salt, something warm to drink. Then one therapist stays with you for the whole hour, working to the pressure you agreed, checking it once, and reading the rest from the muscle.

  4. Afterwards

    The plunge pool and the jacuzzi are a few steps from the door and both are yours to use: cold water first, then warm. Ten minutes of it settles what the treatment has loosened, in the quietest corner of the building.

The rooms, and what is in the bottles.

Two treatment rooms, one of them built for couples.

One room takes two, side by side, which is why couples and friends book it together and why it is the first to go at a weekend. The other is set for a single treatment. Timber, stone, low light, and the water audible through the window in both.

The oils are Ayurvedic and pressed here in Sri Lanka rather than shipped in: sesame and coconut as the base, with cinnamon, lemongrass, ginger and citronella depending on what you asked for at the start. The facials use botanicals grown on the island. Nothing on the trolley is a bottle with a spa name printed on the label.

And the rest of it.

More reasons to stay in your robe a while longer, included if you are staying and on a day pass if you are not.

The plunge pool and jacuzzi

Cold water first, then warm. Ten minutes of it after a treatment does more than the treatment managed alone, in the quietest courtyard in the building.

The lap pool

It runs the length of the court, so it is a pool to swim in rather than stand in. Loungers on one side, trees over the other, and lunch close enough to order without getting out.

The fitness room

Free weights, a bench, and cardio machines that face the garden rather than a mirror. Small, well kept, and almost never busy, which is the point of a hotel gym.

Sunrise yoga on the roof

Cloud Café before it opens, while the city is still deciding whether to wake up. An hour on the mat as the light arrives, then breakfast downstairs. Arranged on request.

Good to know.

The questions the spa answers most, in case one of them is yours.

Can I use the spa if I am not staying at the hotel?

Yes. The Court Spa is open to anyone, and so is the lap pool on a day pass. Message us and we will tell you what is free.

How far ahead should I book?

A day is usually enough on a weekday. Weekends and evenings go earlier, so give us two or three if you can. We will always tell you honestly what is left.

What should I bring?

Nothing. Robes, slippers and everything else are here. Come a few minutes early, tell us about any injuries or allergies, and leave the rest to the therapist.

Can two people be treated at the same time?

Yes. One of the two treatment rooms is built for couples, with two beds side by side, so you can be treated together. Ask for it when you book, because it is the room that goes first.

How long is a treatment, and how early should I arrive?

Most run to sixty or ninety minutes, and the foot ritual at the start is inside that rather than added to it. Come ten minutes early, so the conversation about pressure and injuries is not rushed and the hour is entirely yours.

What do I wear, and what happens to my clothes?

A robe and slippers are waiting in the room, and there is a locker for everything else. Wear as much or as little under the towel as you are comfortable in; the therapist works around it and you are covered throughout.

Can I have a treatment while I am pregnant?

Yes, after the first trimester, with a therapist trained for it and a different position on the bed. Tell us when you book rather than when you arrive, so the right person and the right oils are ready for you.

Is there anywhere to sit afterwards?

That is rather the point of the courtyard. Robe, tea, the plunge pool and the jacuzzi, and a chair in the shade. Nobody will move you along, and the day pass includes all of it.
