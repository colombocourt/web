Colombo Court / Wellness

Restore & reconnect.

Thoughtful treatments, quiet spaces and simple ways to feel more like yourself.

Book a treatment

What to expect.

  1. You arrive

    Your Balinese therapist welcomes you and takes a moment to understand how you are feeling. Tell them where you feel tension, any injuries or sensitivities, and the level of pressure you prefer. This helps shape the treatment around you.

  2. What suits you

    Choose from Balinese or warm stone massages, body scrubs and facials, with treatments ranging from 30 to 120 minutes. Your therapist can guide you towards the treatment and duration that best suit how you are feeling that day.

  3. The treatment

    Your treatment begins with a moment to settle in before your therapist gets to work. Throughout the experience, they stay attentive to your comfort, adjusting the pressure and technique to what you discussed together at the beginning.

  4. Afterwards

    When your treatment is complete, take your time before returning to the day. The plunge pool and jacuzzi are just steps away, offering a chance to move between cool and warm water and let the feeling of relaxation continue a little longer.

Our treatment rooms and oils.

Our double treatment room has two beds side by side, making it ideal for couples or friends who would like to experience a treatment together. A second room is available for individual treatments. Both spaces combine natural timber, stone and soft lighting, with the gentle sound of water creating a calm atmosphere.

Our treatments use natural oils, including pure virgin coconut oil from Sri Lanka and carefully selected essential oil blends. Facials incorporate natural ingredients such as honey and cucumber, chosen to complement the treatment and leave your skin feeling refreshed.

And the rest of it.

The plunge pool and jacuzzi

Move between cool and warm water after your treatment and give yourself a little more time to unwind. Set quietly within the garden, the plunge pool and jacuzzi offer a peaceful place to pause before returning to your day.

The pool

Stretch out with a proper swim along the length of the garden pool, then settle into a lounger beneath the trees. When hunger calls, Amber Poolside is close by for something to eat, a refreshing drink or a relaxed lunch.

The fitness room

Keep your routine going with free weights, a bench and cardio equipment overlooking the garden. It is a compact, well-kept space designed for simple workouts, with a peaceful view and an atmosphere that rarely feels crowded.

A rooftop for sunrise yoga

As the city begins to wake, the rooftop at Cloud Café & Bar offers an open-air setting for an early morning yoga session. Speak with our team about what you need, and we will explore the arrangements available for your stay.

Wellness, answered.

Can I use the spa if I am not staying at the hotel?

Yes. The Court Spa welcomes both hotel guests and visitors from 9am to 9pm daily. Non-residents can also enjoy the pool through our Dine & Dip experience. Please contact us to check availability.

How far ahead should I book?

A day ahead is generally enough on weekdays. Weekends and evenings can be busier, so we recommend booking two or three days ahead whenever possible, particularly if you have a preferred treatment or time.

What should I bring?

Nothing special. Robes, slippers and everything you need for your treatment are provided. Please arrive 10 minutes early and let your therapist know about any injuries, allergies or medication before your treatment begins.

Can two people be treated at the same time?

Yes. Our double treatment room has two beds side by side, allowing couples or friends to enjoy their treatments together. As this room is popular, we recommend requesting it when making your reservation.

How long is a treatment, and how early should I arrive?

Treatments range from 30 to 120 minutes, with most full-body massages taking 60 or 90 minutes. Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment so you have time to settle in and speak with your therapist.

What do I wear?

A robe and slippers will be ready for you in the treatment room. You will also have access to a locker for your personal belongings, so you can settle into your treatment comfortably without needing to bring anything with you.

Is there anywhere to sit afterwards?

Yes. Take your time after your treatment and relax in the poolside garden with a cup of tea. You are also welcome to enjoy the plunge pool and jacuzzi, giving you space to stay in that relaxed state for a little longer.
