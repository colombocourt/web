Colombo Court / Facilities

A small hotel
with nothing left out.

Airport transfers, free parking, a lap pool and a gym open from 07:00, and a quiet library.

Start with arriving Check dates

Arriving

Someone will be at arrivals with your name.

Bandaranaike International is about 35 kilometres north of the hotel, which is forty-five minutes to an hour depending on the traffic. Send us your flight number and we will arrange a private, air-conditioned car at any hour, with one of our regular drivers waiting for you in arrivals. We also arrange transfers back to the airport. Please contact our front office for rates.

If you are driving, parking is free and on site at 32 Alfred House Avenue, for house guests and for anyone dining with us. Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is at 12:00 noon. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and chargeable.

  • 35 km from the airport
  • Any hour for an airport car
  • 14:00 check-in, 12:00 check-out
  • Free parking on site

Settling in

Everything in the room, and one call for the rest.

Every room, whichever you take, has the same things waiting in it: Wi-Fi that reaches every corner, a Smart TV, a minibar, coffee and tea, a safe, a rain shower and a hair dryer, with bottled water set out fresh each day. Tell us whether you sleep best on micro gel, feather or foam, and that is the pillow you will find on the bed.

For everything else, one call is all it takes. The phone in your room connects you directly to a member of our team, who can send up dinner, bring an iron, or book a car or a table for you.

  • Wi-Fi throughout
  • Smart TV
  • Minibar
  • Coffee and tea
  • In-room safe
  • Rain shower
  • Hair dryer
  • Bath amenities
  • Three pillow types
  • Shoe shine
  • Bottled water daily
  • In-room dining

The Luxury Junior Suites, the Luxury Honeymoon Suite and the Luxury Duplex Suite add a bath and a Nescafé Dolce Gusto machine, and have an iron and board in the room; in every other room they arrive on request. Smoking is not permitted in any room, and accessible rooms are available on prior request. See the rooms

Mornings

Pool and gym from 07:00.

The lap pool, the jacuzzi and the gym are open from 07:00 to 20:00, and free for house guests throughout their stay.

  • 07:00 to 20:00

    The lap pool

    It runs the length of the garden under the trees and is long enough for proper lengths. Early in the morning you may have it to yourself.

  • Beside the spa

    The plunge pool

    Small and cool, set in front of a carved arched door. Ideal after a workout or a long flight.

  • 07:00 to 20:00

    The jacuzzi

    Warm water and low light. The plunge pool is a few steps away, so you can move between cool and warm water.

  • 07:00 to 20:00

    The gym

    Free weights, cardio machines and a window on to the garden. Small, well kept and rarely busy.

The quiet hours

A library, and its National Geographics.

Off the garden, behind a wall of glass, is a library of 140 square feet with floor to ceiling shelves, deep leather armchairs, a table that seats eight and plenty of National Geographic magazines. It is for house guests, it is rarely busy, and it is a pleasant place to spend an hour after lunch.

It is also our quietest meeting room, with a door you can close for a call or a conversation and room for eight around the table.

The Court Spa is a few steps away, with Balinese therapists, treatments from reflexology to full-body massage, and the plunge pool and jacuzzi afterwards. It is open daily from 9am to 9pm.

While you are here

The practical things, done properly.

The everyday services that make your stay easier.

  • Laundry and dry cleaning

    Washing, pressing and dry cleaning, collected from your room. Tell us when you need it back and we will say straight away whether that can be done.

  • A concierge who knows Colombo

    Our team can book tables, arrange cars and recommend the best ways to spend an afternoon in Colombo.

  • Dinner in the room

    The same kitchen that cooks for Amber Poolside and Cloud Café sends food up to the room. Ask for the in-room menu, or simply ask for what you want.

  • An iron, a board, a shoe shine

    An iron and board are in every suite and are brought to any other room on request. Shoes left out for a shine come back ready for the evening.

  • A room for work

    The Scarlet Room holds up to thirty with a projector and screen, and the library seats eight with no screen at all. Both can be booked for a half day or a full day.

  • Wi-Fi everywhere

    Complimentary throughout the hotel, from the rooms to the garden and the rooftop, with no codes and no extra charge.

Behind the scenes, we use solar water heating, collect rainwater, fit LED lighting and use recycled paper. Read more on our Sustainability page.

Before you arrive.

The questions the front desk answers most, in case one of them is yours.

Is parking free at Colombo Court?

Yes. Parking is free and on site at 32 Alfred House Avenue in Colombo 3, for house guests and for anyone dining with us.

How do I get from the airport to the hotel?

Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) is about 35 km away, which is 45 minutes to an hour by car depending on traffic. We arrange private transfers at any hour on request. Please contact our front office for rates. Send your flight number when you book and a driver will meet you in arrivals.

What time are the pool and the gym open?

The lap pool, the jacuzzi and the gym are open from 07:00 to 20:00, and they are free for house guests throughout their stay.

Is there a library, and who can use it?

Yes. There is a small library off the garden with floor to ceiling shelves, leather armchairs, a table for eight and plenty of National Geographic magazines. It is for house guests, and it can also be booked as a quiet meeting room for up to eight.

Do you offer laundry and dry cleaning?

Yes, along with pressing. Ask at the front desk or through the one-call service in your room, and we will tell you when it can be back.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is at 12:00 noon. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and chargeable, so ask when you book.

Is the Wi-Fi free?

Yes. Wi-Fi is complimentary throughout the hotel, in every room and in the public spaces.

Is there a spa at the hotel?

Yes. The Court Spa is open daily from 9am to 9pm, with Balinese therapists and treatments from reflexology to full-body massage, followed by the plunge pool and the jacuzzi.

Can I work from the hotel?

Yes. Every room has a reading table and Wi-Fi, the library seats eight around one table, and the Scarlet Room holds up to thirty with a projector and screen for a half day or a full day.

Are the rooms non-smoking?

Yes. All thirty-one rooms and suites are non-smoking.

Do you have accessible rooms?

Yes. Accessible rooms and facilities are available on prior request. Please let us know your requirements when you book.

Which cards and currencies do you accept?

We accept American Express, Visa and Mastercard. We also accept US dollars, British pounds, Australian dollars, euros and Sri Lankan rupees.

Can I bring my pet?

We're unable to accommodate pets at the hotel. If you have any questions, please contact us before you book.
