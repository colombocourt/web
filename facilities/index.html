Colombo Court / Facilities

The details matter.

Thoughtful facilities and simple comforts, designed to make your stay easier.

Start with arriving Check dates

Arriving

From arrival to departure.

Bandaranaike International Airport is around 35 kilometres from the hotel, usually 45 minutes to an hour depending on traffic. Share your flight details and we can arrange a private, air-conditioned vehicle with one of our regular drivers waiting in arrivals. Transfers back to the airport can also be arranged.

If you are driving, complimentary on-site parking is available at 32 Alfred House Avenue for hotel guests and dining guests, subject to availability. Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is at 12:00 noon. Early check-in and late check-out can be arranged, subject to availability and applicable charges.

Settling in

Everything in your room.

Every room comes with the essentials for an easy stay, including Wi-Fi, a Smart TV, minibar, tea and coffee facilities, safe, rain shower, hair dryer and fresh bottled water each day. You can also choose between micro-gel, feather or foam pillows, so you can settle in just the way you like.

For everything else, your room phone connects you directly with our team. Need dinner, an iron, a car or a restaurant reservation? Just call, and we will take care of it.

Outdoors

Pool, gym & more.

  • The pool

    Set among the garden and trees, our pool is a peaceful place for an early swim, a refreshing dip or a few quiet hours beside the water.

  • The jacuzzi

    Warm water, gentle bubbles and a quiet garden setting make the jacuzzi an easy place to unwind after a day in the city.

  • The gym

    A compact fitness room with free weights, cardio equipment and a view of the garden, covering the essentials for keeping up your routine.

  • The garden

    Greenery surrounds the heart of the hotel, giving you space to walk, sit quietly or simply spend time outdoors away from Colombo’s busy streets.

The quiet hours

A library with a past.

Behind a wall of glass beside the garden, our library is a small, quiet space with floor-to-ceiling shelves, deep leather armchairs and a table for eight. It is filled with books, old magazines and almost a century of National Geographic issues, giving you plenty to browse when you feel like putting your phone away.

The library is available to hotel guests and is rarely busy, making it an easy place to spend an hour after breakfast or lunch, read, catch up on some work or simply enjoy the quiet. The table can also be used for small private conversations or meetings, with the door closed when you need a little more privacy.

While you are here

The practical things, done properly.

  • Laundry & dry cleaning

    Have your clothes washed, pressed or dry cleaned without leaving the hotel. Collection is available from your room, and our team will confirm the expected return time when you make the request.

  • A concierge who knows Colombo

    Need a restaurant recommendation, a car for the afternoon or somewhere interesting to explore? Our team can help arrange your plans and share suggestions based on what you enjoy.

  • Dinner in your room

    Enjoy dishes from the same kitchen that serves Amber Poolside and Cloud Café & Bar. Ask for the in-room menu, or speak with our team if there is something specific you would like.

  • An iron, a board & shoe shine

    Iron and ironing boards are provided in every Suite and can be brought to other rooms on request. Leave your shoes with us for a little polish before heading out for the evening.

  • A room for work

    For meetings and focused work, the Scarlet Room accommodates up to 30 guests with a projector and screen. The Library offers a quieter setting for smaller groups of up to eight. Both can be booked by the half day or full day.

  • Turndown service

    Return in the evening to a room prepared for the night. Our team can refresh the space, draw the curtains and make sure everything is comfortable before you settle in.

Behind the scenes, we use solar water heating, collect rainwater, fit LED lighting and use recycled paper. Read more on our Sustainability page.

Before you arrive.

Is Wi-Fi available throughout the hotel?

Yes. Complimentary Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel, including guest rooms and common areas.

Is parking available?

Yes. Complimentary on-site parking is available for hotel guests and dining guests, subject to availability.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is at 12:00 noon. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and applicable charges.

Can I use the pool if I am not staying at the hotel?

Yes, through our Dine & Dip experience, subject to availability and the applicable package terms.

Is there a gym at the hotel?

Yes. Our fitness room includes free weights and cardio equipment and is available to hotel guests from 07:00 to 20:00.

Can you arrange airport transfers?

Yes. We can arrange private airport transfers in an air-conditioned vehicle. Please share your flight details with our Front Office team for rates and arrangements.
