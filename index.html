Your Urban Hideaway

09:20a house, not a hotel

A boutique hotel built
around a garden.

Thirty-one rooms and suites look onto a garden with a lap pool running its length. The garden sits at the centre of the hotel, away from the noise of the street. We are on Alfred House Avenue in Colombo 3, a few minutes from the sea and from many of the city’s best restaurants.

Inside, it feels more like a home than a hotel. You will find art by Sri Lankan artists, reclaimed timber, and drinking water filtered and bottled in glass on site. Our team gives personal service, so by your second morning someone will know how you take your coffee.

09:00wellness

Slow down.

A spa, two pools, a gym, and a rooftop made for sunrise yoga.

The Court Spa is open daily from 9am to 9pm. Our Balinese therapists offer deep tissue and aromatherapy massage with Ayurvedic oils pressed in Sri Lanka, and facials made with local botanicals. Every treatment begins with a foot ritual and a warm drink, and there is no need to rush afterwards.

Afterwards you can enjoy the plunge pool and the jacuzzi, the lap pool in the garden, and a fitness room that faces the garden. The Cloud Café roof is an ideal place for yoga at sunrise. Tell us what you need and we will look at the options for you.

A hotel that treads lightly.

Colombo Court was the first city hotel in South Asia to be certified CarbonNeutral®.

We reduce our impact in practical ways every day. We filter and bottle drinking water in glass on site, recycle water for the garden, separate and weigh our waste, and use reclaimed timber.

Good to know.

The questions that arrive most often, answered plainly. Anything else, just message us.

What are your check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is at 12:00 noon. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and chargeable, so message us on WhatsApp before you travel and we will arrange it if we can.

How far is the airport from the hotel?

Bandaranaike International Airport is about 35 km north of Colombo. By expressway the drive takes roughly 45 minutes to an hour, depending on the time of day. Colombo Court is in Colombo 03, so you arrive in the middle of the city rather than on its edge.

Do you provide airport pick-up and drop-off?

Yes. We arrange private airport transfers at any hour, in an air-conditioned car with one of our regular drivers. Please contact our front office for rates. Send us your flight number when you book and a driver will meet you in arrivals.

Can non-residents eat at the hotel?

Yes. Amber Poolside serves breakfast, lunch from 12:30 and high tea beside the pool until 18:30, and Cloud Café & Bar on the roof is open from 17:30 to 23:30 every day. Booking a table is a good idea at weekends.

Is there parking at the hotel?

Yes. Parking on site is free for house guests and for anyone dining with us. Space is limited, so please let us know in advance if you are arriving with more than one car.

Can guests use the pool and the spa?

The lap pool, the jacuzzi and the gym are free for house guests throughout their stay. The Court Spa is open from 9am to 9pm daily for house guests and visitors, and we recommend booking a treatment a day ahead.

Is Colombo Court a good base for a first visit to Sri Lanka?

Yes, it is a good first and last stop. The hotel is in Colombo 3, close to the airport road, the Galle Face seafront, the museums and many of the best restaurants. The rooms face the garden, away from the traffic. Many guests stay two nights at each end of their trip, and we are happy to store luggage while they travel.

Do you have a lift, and is the hotel accessible for wheelchair users?

There is a lift to every floor, including the rooftop. The ground floor, the garden and both restaurants are step-free. Accessible rooms and facilities are available on prior request, so please tell us your requirements when you book.

What is the best time of year to visit Colombo?

December to March is the drier, cooler stretch and the busiest. April to June and September to November bring short heavy showers that clear quickly, lower rates and a much quieter hotel. Colombo is warm all year; nothing here closes for a season.

Can I bring my pet?

We're unable to accommodate pets at the hotel. If you have any questions, please contact us before you book.

What is your cancellation and payment policy?

For stays between 20 December and 31 March, please give 14 days' written notice before arrival to cancel. For all other dates, please give 24 hours' written notice before arrival. A payment of 50% of the booking value confirms your reservation, and the balance is paid on arrival. For stays between 20 December and 31 March, the balance is due 14 days before arrival.

Do children stay free?

Children aged 5 and under stay free of charge and may share their parents’ bed, or have an extra bed for USD 15 per night. Children aged 6 to 11 pay 50% of the meal rate and need an extra bed at USD 15 per night. Guests aged 12 and over pay the full rate.

Which cards and currencies do you accept?

We accept American Express, Visa and Mastercard. We also accept US dollars, British pounds, Australian dollars, euros and Sri Lankan rupees.
