09:20a house, not a hotel

A boutique hotel built
around a garden.

Thirty-one rooms and suites look down into a planted court with a lap pool running its length. The quiet part is in the middle, and the city stays outside the hedge. It sits on Alfred House Avenue in Colombo 3, a few minutes from the sea and from most of the places you will want to eat.

Inside, it behaves like a house that happens to take guests. Art by Sri Lankan hands, timber reclaimed rather than felled, water filtered and bottled in glass a few steps from your room. The team is small on purpose, so by the second morning someone already knows how you take your coffee.

10:00wellness at the court

Slow down.

A spa, two pools, a gym, and yoga on the roof at sunrise.

The Court Spa is where guests come to put the day down. Balinese therapists, Ayurvedic oils pressed here in Sri Lanka, deep tissue and aromatherapy, and facials made with local botanicals. Every treatment opens the same way, with a foot ritual and something warm to drink, and ends whenever you are ready to move again.

Afterwards there is the plunge pool and the jacuzzi, the lap pool running the length of the court, and a fitness room that faces the garden. Yoga on the Cloud Café roof is arranged on request, for whichever morning you want it.

Discover Colombo

A hotel that treads lightly.

In 2011 Colombo Court became South Asia’s first city hotel certified CarbonNeutral®. We have held it every year since, which is the harder part.

It shows up in small ways rather than announcements. Drinking water filtered and bottled in glass on site. Water recycled back into the garden. Waste separated and weighed. Timber reclaimed rather than felled.

Good to know.

The questions that arrive most often, answered plainly. Anything else, just message us.

What are your check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is at 12:00 noon. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and chargeable, so message us on WhatsApp before you travel and we will arrange it if we can.

How far is the airport from the hotel?

Bandaranaike International Airport is about 35 km north of Colombo. By expressway the drive takes roughly 45 minutes to an hour, depending on the time of day. Colombo Court is in Colombo 03, so you arrive in the middle of the city rather than on its edge.

Do you provide airport pick-up and drop-off?

Yes. We arrange private airport transfers at any hour, in an air-conditioned car with one of our regular drivers. Tell us your flight number when you book and someone will be waiting in arrivals with your name.

Can non-residents eat at the hotel?

Yes. Amber Poolside serves breakfast, lunch from 12:30 and high tea beside the pool, and Cloud Café & Bar on the roof is open to 23:30 every day. Booking a table is a good idea at weekends.

Is there parking at the hotel?

Yes, complimentary on-site parking for house guests and for anyone dining with us. Space is limited, so let us know in advance if you are arriving with more than one car.

Can guests use the pool and the spa?

The lap pool, the jacuzzi and the gym are open to house guests throughout their stay at no charge. Court Spa treatments are bookable by house guests and by visitors, and we recommend reserving a treatment a day ahead.

Is Colombo Court a good base for a first visit to Sri Lanka?

It is a sensible first and last stop. You are in Colombo 3, so the airport road, the Galle Face seafront, the museums and the best of the eating are all close, and the garden means you are not sleeping beside traffic. Most guests spend two nights here at each end of a trip and let us hold a bag while they travel.

Do you have a lift, and is the hotel workable for a wheelchair?

There is a lift to every floor, including the rooftop. The ground floor, the courtyard and both restaurants are step-free. A few older thresholds are not, so tell us before you book and we will put you in a room that works and walk the route with you.

What is the best time of year to visit Colombo?

December to March is the drier, cooler stretch and the busiest. April to June and September to November bring short heavy showers that clear quickly, lower rates and a much quieter hotel. Colombo is warm all year; nothing here closes for a season.

Do you take dogs?

Not in the rooms, and we would rather say so plainly than have you arrive and find out. Assistance dogs are welcome throughout, and we will happily recommend a boarding kennel we trust.
