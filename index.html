Your Urban Hideaway

A quiet retreat in the
heart of Colombo.

Hidden behind the energy of Colombo 3, Colombo Court Hotel & Spa unfolds around a lush garden and pool, creating a rare sense of calm in the heart of the city. Rooms and Suites give the hotel an intimate character, with individually considered spaces, Sri Lankan art, reclaimed timber and thoughtful details that make no two stays feel quite the same.

Spend the morning among the greenery, take a quiet moment by the pool, or step beyond the hotel to discover Colombo’s restaurants, culture and coastline. Then return to a place that feels personal, peaceful and distinctly its own.

Your wellbeing.

Wellness at Colombo Court is about having space to feel your best, in your own way. Begin the day with a refreshing swim, enjoy a treatment at The Court Spa, keep up your fitness routine or simply take time to enjoy the garden and its peaceful surroundings. Our Balinese therapists offer massages and facials using locally sourced ingredients, while the fitness room overlooks the greenery.

For a more invigorating experience, take a dip in the pool or unwind in the plunge pool and jacuzzi. The rooftop at Cloud Café & Bar offers a beautiful setting for yoga, particularly as the city begins to wake. Whether you want to recharge, stay active or simply make more time for yourself, wellness is naturally woven into your stay.

Good to know.

What are your check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is at 12:00 noon. If you would like to arrive earlier or enjoy a little more time before you leave, early check-in and late check-out can be arranged subject to availability and applicable charges. Simply contact our team before your arrival and we will be happy to check what is possible.

How far is Colombo Court Hotel from the airport?

Bandaranaike International Airport is approximately 35 km from Colombo Court Hotel & Spa. The journey usually takes around 45 minutes to 1 hour by expressway, depending on traffic and time of day. Located in Colombo 03, the hotel places you within easy reach of the city’s coastline, restaurants, shopping, culture and business districts, making it a convenient beginning or end to your Sri Lankan journey.

Do you provide airport transfers?

Yes. We can arrange a private airport transfer in an air-conditioned vehicle at any time of day or night. Our regular drivers provide a comfortable and personal arrival experience, with your driver meeting you at the arrivals area. Simply share your flight details with us when you make your reservation and our team will arrange the rest. Please contact our Front Office for current rates.

Can non-residents dine at Colombo Court?

Absolutely. You do not need to be staying with us to enjoy our dining experiences.

Amber Poolside
Breakfast: 07:00 onwards
Lunch: 12:00 onwards
High Tea: 15:00 to 18:30

Cloud Café & Bar
Dinner and drinks: 17:30 to 23:30

From a relaxed breakfast beside the garden and pool to an evening above the city at Cloud Café & Bar, our dining spaces are open for both hotel guests and visitors. Reservations are recommended, particularly for weekends and special occasions. Make a reservation.

Is parking available at the hotel?

Yes. Complimentary on-site parking is available for hotel guests and guests dining with us. As parking spaces are limited, we recommend informing us in advance if you will be arriving with more than one vehicle so we can assist with arrangements.

Can I use the pool, gym and spa if I am not staying at the hotel?

Our hotel guests can enjoy the pool, jacuzzi and gym throughout their stay. The Court Spa is open daily from 09:00 to 21:00 for both hotel guests and visitors, with treatments best reserved in advance.

If you are visiting Colombo and would like to enjoy the hotel without staying overnight, our Dine & Dip experience allows you to make a day of it.

Dine & Dip
LKR 3,500 net per person. Includes access to the pool, and the full amount is redeemable on food and beverage. It is an easy way to spend a relaxed afternoon surrounded by the garden, pool and calm atmosphere of Colombo Court. Book a Dine & Dip day.

Is Colombo Court a good place to stay at the beginning or end of a Sri Lankan journey?

Colombo Court is designed to be more than simply a place to spend your first or last night in Sri Lanka. Our location in Colombo 03 puts you at the centre of the city, making it easy to experience the different sides of Colombo during your stay.

Explore the coastline, discover local restaurants and cafés, visit museums and cultural attractions, enjoy shopping, attend a business meeting, or simply spend an afternoon by the pool. Medical facilities, shopping areas, entertainment and many of Colombo’s everyday conveniences are also within easy reach.

After a day in the city, return to the quiet of our garden, where the atmosphere feels distinctly removed from the energy outside. Whether you are beginning your Sri Lankan adventure, returning before your flight home or staying a little longer to experience Colombo itself, the hotel gives you a comfortable and central place to start, explore and return to.

Is Colombo Court suitable for business travellers?

Yes. Our Colombo 03 location provides convenient access to Colombo’s main business areas while giving business travellers a quieter place to return to at the end of the day.

With comfortable rooms and Suites, complimentary Wi-Fi, dining options within the hotel, wellness facilities and spaces to work or unwind, Colombo Court offers a more personal alternative to a conventional business hotel. For longer stays or important business trips, our Suites also provide additional space, privacy and comfort.

How far is Colombo Court from Colombo city centre?

Colombo Court is located in Colombo 03, one of the city’s most central and convenient neighbourhoods. Rather than being separated from the city, the hotel places you within easy reach of Colombo’s key experiences, including the seafront, restaurants, shopping, business districts, cultural attractions and entertainment.

The hotel itself offers a peaceful retreat around its garden and pool, giving you the best of both sides: the city when you want to explore it, and a quiet place to return to afterwards.

Does Colombo Court have an elevator and is the hotel wheelchair accessible?

The hotel has an elevator providing access to the rooftop restaurant. Guest rooms are accessed via the stairways.

The ground floor, garden and restaurant areas are step-free, and accessible arrangements can be discussed in advance. If you have specific mobility requirements, please let us know when making your reservation so our team can advise you on the most suitable arrangements for your stay.

What is the best time of year to visit Colombo?

December to March has traditionally been considered the most favourable period to visit Colombo, with generally drier and cooler conditions.

However, Colombo has evolved into a year-round destination, and changing travel patterns mean there is no single season in which the city needs to be experienced. The city remains lively throughout the year, with dining, shopping, culture, wellness and activities available in every season.

Whether you visit during the traditional high season or at another time of year, Colombo Court offers a garden retreat where you can enjoy the city at your own pace.

Do you serve buffets?

Buffet service is available depending on hotel occupancy and the day’s dining requirements. On days when a buffet is offered, guests can enjoy a broader selection of dishes and a more relaxed dining experience. Our team can confirm the dining arrangement for your dates when you make your reservation.

Are extra beds or baby cots available?

Yes. We can arrange baby cots free of charge, subject to availability.

Extra beds are available for USD 15 per night, subject to room configuration and availability. If you are travelling with children, please let us know when making your reservation so we can prepare the room according to your requirements.

Are children welcome at Colombo Court?

Yes. We welcome families and have room options that can provide additional space and comfort.

Infants to 5 years: stay free of charge, including meals.
6 to 12 years: 50% of the applicable meal rate, plus an extra bed charge of USD 15 per night.
Above 12 years: considered an adult and charged at the applicable adult rate.

Please let us know the ages of your children when making your reservation so we can recommend the most suitable room or Suite.

Can I bring my pet?

Unfortunately, we are not a pet-friendly hotel and are unable to accommodate pets on the property. If you have any questions about your stay or special requirements, please contact our team before booking.

What is your cancellation and payment policy?

Our cancellation and payment conditions vary depending on the time of year. To keep things simple, here is how they work:

Off season (all other dates)
A 24-hour cancellation notice is required before arrival.
Cancellations made within the applicable period are refundable according to the booking terms.

Season (20 December to 31 March, and July and August)
A 14-day cancellation notice is required before arrival.
Cancellations made 14 days or more before arrival are refundable according to the booking terms.
Cancellations made within 14 days of arrival are non-refundable.

Payment
Booking confirmation requires a 50% advance payment.
The remaining balance is payable according to the applicable booking terms and season.

Your reservation confirmation will always state the specific cancellation and payment conditions applicable to your stay. If you have a question about your booking, ask our reception team.

Which cards and currencies do you accept?

We accept American Express, Visa and Mastercard.

For your convenience, we also accept payments in:

US Dollars (USD)
British Pounds (GBP)
Australian Dollars (AUD)
Euros (EUR)

Currency conversion is subject to the applicable exchange rate at the time of payment.
