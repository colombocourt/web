Put the big picture that goes at the top of the post in the "header" folder.
Put the pictures that go in the middle of the post in the "inside" folder.

Name each file for what it is, in lower case with dashes and no spaces:

   ceylon-tea.jpg
   tea-plantation.jpg

Then double-click add-pictures.cmd in the folder above this one.

Use the biggest original you have. A header picture needs to be at least
1600 pixels wide; an inside picture at least 1100.
