Your first spa visit, and what nobody tells you

What to wear, what happens to your clothes, how much pressure to ask for and whether to talk. The awkward questions, answered plainly, so the hour is not spent wondering.

Wellness 8 July 2026 5 minute read

A great many people have never had a massage and are slightly embarrassed to ask any of the following. All of it is normal, all of it is asked constantly, and none of it should cost you an hour of quiet wondering.

What do I wear?

A robe and slippers are waiting in the room and there is a locker for everything else. Under the towel, wear as much or as little as you are comfortable in. Disposable underwear is provided if you would rather use it. You are covered throughout, with only the part being worked on uncovered, and the therapist will move the towel for you rather than ask you to.

Do I have to talk?

No. There is a conversation before the treatment, about what hurts and how much pressure you want, and after that silence is entirely normal and most people prefer it. If you would like to talk, that is fine too. A good therapist will follow your lead within about ninety seconds.

How much pressure should I ask for?

More than you think, and say so early. The single most common thing we hear afterwards is that somebody wished they had asked for firmer and did not want to make a fuss. It is not a fuss. Say “firmer” or “lighter” at any point and it changes immediately.

Pressure should be intense and never sharp. Discomfort in a tight muscle is the work happening. Pain is not, and you should say so.

The practical things

Arrive ten minutes early, so the conversation is not rushed and the hour is entirely yours.

Do not eat a heavy meal first. An hour face down after lunch is nobody’s best idea.

Say if you are pregnant, injured, recovering from surgery, on blood thinners or allergic to nuts. Almost every massage oil has a nut base, and this one genuinely matters.

The foot ritual at the start is part of the treatment, not an extra, and it is inside the time rather than added to it.

Afterwards

Drink water, and do not book anything. The mistake is scheduling a treatment an hour before dinner. The good part is the hour after, and at The Court Spa the plunge pool and the jacuzzi are a few steps from the treatment room door. Cold water then warm does more than the massage managed on its own.

What to book first

Sixty minutes of Balinese, and firm. It is the one where you feel something happen, which is what you want the first time. We have written about the difference if you would like to choose properly.
