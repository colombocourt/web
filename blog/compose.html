Write a blog post

Paste the words, choose the pictures, press the button. This page writes the formatting for you; it does not publish anything on its own.

Do the pictures first. Rename them, drop them into blog\_new-pictures\header and blog\_new-pictures\inside, and double-click blog\add-pictures.cmd. Then come back here and their names will be waiting in the Main picture box.

The post

Never a future date.

/blog/…/ — leave it alone unless you have a reason.

Pictures

Use the file name without the size, so bl-roof, not bl-roof-700.jpg. The main picture must be one of the -hero images, which are built wide enough for a header; start typing and the list will offer them. The second and third can be any picture in assets/img/. Leave them blank if the post has none. If you name one that does not exist, publishing stops and tells you which.

The words

Blank line between paragraphs. ## for a sub-heading. **bold** for bold. [image 2] and [image 3] place the pictures. [words](/link/) makes a link.

Then
  1. Press Copy it.
  2. Open blog\_src\posts.txt in Notepad and paste it at the very bottom. Save.
  3. Double-click blog\publish.cmd.
  4. Upload assets\img, blog and sitemap.xml into public_html on Hostinger, replacing what is there.

Step three rewrites the listing, the new post’s own page, every other post’s next and previous links, the feed and the sitemap, all together, so none of them can disagree.
