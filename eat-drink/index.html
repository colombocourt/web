When to come, and where to sit.

Amber Poolside is our daytime restaurant, beside the pool in the shade of the trees. Cloud Café is our evening restaurant and bar on the rooftop, with a glass roof over part of it and views across the city. Both are open to everyone, not only hotel guests.

Our chefs are happy to cook to your taste: a favourite dish, a dietary need, a special table or a birthday cake. Tell us when you book and we will have it ready.

Cloud Café & Bar

A calm rooftop restaurant and bar with views across the city.

Our newly refurbished rooftop has a glass roof over part of it, trees in planters, a bar faced in old Ceylon cement tiles, and views across the city.

Open every evening from 17:30 to 23:30. Enjoy cocktails, a chosen wine list and a semi fine dining menu of pizza, pasta, salads, and Indian and Western dishes. For a special evening, our chef can create a menu for your table.

Before you come.

Answers to the questions our restaurant team is asked most often.

Do I have to be staying at the hotel?

No. Both restaurants are open to everyone. Walk in for breakfast or lunch at the pool, or book a table for dinner and drinks on the roof.

What time is breakfast, and how late is high tea?

Breakfast is served beside the pool from 07:00 to 10:30 every day. Lunch starts at 12:30, and high tea is served at Amber Poolside from 15:30 to 18:30. Non-residents are welcome.

Where exactly are you, and what is nearby?

Colombo Court Hotel & Spa, 32 Alfred House Avenue, Colombo 3. We are a few minutes from Galle Face Green, Gangaramaya Temple and the shops on Duplication Road, and a short drive from Fort.

Is there parking, and is the roof easy to reach?

Yes. Parking is free on site at 32 Alfred House Avenue, and there is a lift as well as stairs up to Cloud Café.

Do you take walk-ins, or should I book?

Yes, you can walk in to either restaurant. Weekends are busy and the tables along the rail are popular, so we recommend booking ahead with the table request form on this page.

Is there a dress code?

There is no formal dress code. Casual wear is fine at the pool, and smart casual suits the roof in the evening.

Can you build a menu around what we want?

Yes. Our chef can create a menu for your table. Tell us what your guests like, what they avoid and what the occasion is, and the chef will plan a menu for you. A day or two of notice is usually enough, and we share the menu with you before you confirm.

Can you work around allergies, and is there enough for vegetarians?

Yes. Both menus include vegetarian and vegan dishes. Please tell us about any allergies when you book, so the kitchen can prepare for you.

Can you seat a larger group?

Yes. We can arrange set menus and sharing platters for larger tables in advance. For a gathering with a guest list, our events team will be happy to help.

Can we take the roof for a private evening?

Yes. We can reserve part or all of the roof, depending on the date and the size of your group. Send us the date and number of guests, and we will confirm what is possible. The chef will then plan a menu for your evening.

Are children welcome?

Yes, children are very welcome. High chairs are available on request, and children usually enjoy being near the pool at Amber Poolside.

We look forward to welcoming you.
