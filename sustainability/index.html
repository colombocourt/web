Colombo Court / Sustainability

Caring for our city
and our planet.

The first city hotel in South Asia to be certified CarbonNeutral. Here is how we care for energy, water and waste, and how we give back to nature.

About our certification Our Story

Our certification

The first CarbonNeutral city hotel in South Asia.

Colombo Court was the first city hotel in South Asia to be certified CarbonNeutral®. We are proud of this achievement and of the daily work behind it.

Like every city hotel, we use energy for air conditioning, lighting and cooking. Our approach is simple. We measure what the building uses, reduce it wherever we can, and offset the rest.

  • Certified CarbonNeutral®
  • 1st city hotel in South Asia to achieve it
  • Reduce energy and water use
  • Offset the remaining emissions

Colombo Court has also been named Luxury Green Hotel, Indian Ocean, at the World Luxury Hotel Awards.

The building

A hotel made from existing buildings.

The hotel was created by converting old office buildings on Alfred House Avenue instead of demolishing them. This avoided much of the concrete, steel and transport that a new building would need. Our Story tells you more.

Many of the materials we added have been reused. They include salvaged brick, reclaimed timber, bar stools in the Loft made from old tractor seats, and sculptures made from scrap metal by the Sri Lankan artist Prageeth Manohansa.

A wall of climbing plants also grows up the tower on Alfred House Avenue, bringing greenery to the street.

Sun, rain and shade

Solar power, rainwater and natural shade.

Hot water in the rooms is heated by solar power. We harvest rainwater, recycle water for the garden, and use LED lighting.

The rooms face inward on to a planted garden. The trees give shade and air moves through the open space, which helps keep the building cool. The garden is part of how the hotel saves energy.

  • Sun heats the water
  • Rain harvested
  • LED lighting
  • Shade from the garden trees

What leaves the building

Reducing waste every day.

Simple daily steps to use less plastic, paper and laundry.

  • Drinking water in glass

    Drinking water is filtered and bottled in glass at the hotel, which reduces the number of plastic bottles we buy. At very busy times, plastic bottles may also be used.

  • Waste separation

    Waste is separated and weighed before it leaves the hotel. This helps us recycle more and reduce what we throw away.

  • Recycled paper

    The paper we use around the hotel, including for menus, bills and confirmations, is recycled.

  • Towel reuse

    Towels are fresh when you arrive and the bathroom is serviced every day. If you would like a towel changed, please leave it on the floor. A card in the bathroom explains more.

Beyond the gate

Giving back to nature.

As part of our corporate social responsibility (CSR), we organise plant giveaways and beach clean-ups. We hold them on World Environment Day and on other occasions when we can give something back to nature.

We also buy food and supplies locally wherever possible, so that more of what our guests spend stays in Sri Lanka.

  • Plant giveaways

    We give away plants on World Environment Day and on other special occasions.

  • Beach clean-ups

    Our team joins beach clean-ups to clear litter from the coast.

  • Local first

    Food and supplies are bought locally wherever possible, supporting Sri Lankan producers.

Frequently asked questions.

Common questions about how the hotel cares for the environment.

Was Colombo Court Hotel & Spa certified carbon neutral?

Colombo Court was the first city hotel in South Asia to be certified CarbonNeutral®.

What does carbon neutral mean for a hotel?

It means the hotel measures its carbon emissions, reduces them wherever possible, and offsets the rest.

Do you use plastic water bottles?

Drinking water is filtered and bottled in glass at the hotel. At very busy times, plastic bottles may also be used.

How does the hotel save water and energy?

Water is heated by solar power, rainwater is harvested, and water is recycled for the garden. The lighting is LED. The rooms face inward on to a planted garden, which keeps the building shaded and cooler.

What is the hotel built from?

The hotel was created by converting old office buildings rather than demolishing them. Much of what was added is salvaged brick and reclaimed timber, and the sculptures are made from scrap metal by the Sri Lankan artist Prageeth Manohansa. The hotel was designed by the environmental architect Sunela Jayewardene.

Has the hotel won awards for sustainability?

Yes. Colombo Court has been named Luxury Green Hotel, Indian Ocean, at the World Luxury Hotel Awards.

Does the hotel do anything beyond its own building?

Yes. As part of our CSR, we hold plant giveaways and beach clean-ups on World Environment Day and on other occasions when we can give something back to nature. We also buy food and supplies locally wherever possible.

Are towels changed every day?

Towels are fresh when you arrive and the bathroom is serviced daily. If you would like a towel changed, please leave it on the floor. A card in the bathroom explains more.
