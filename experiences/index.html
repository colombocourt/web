48 hours in Colombo

Two days, in the order we would do them.

A two-day plan from our local team, with free time in the hottest part of each afternoon.

  1. Day one, morning

    Gangaramaya, then the Fort

    Start early at Gangaramaya Temple, with shoulders and knees covered and shoes left at the door, and walk round to Seema Malaka on Beira Lake. Then take a tuk-tuk to Fort to see the restored Dutch Hospital building and the old clock tower lighthouse.

  2. Day one, afternoon

    Pettah, then the pool

    Pettah is a busy, lively market district. See the red and white Jami Ul-Alfar Mosque, leave before the hottest part of the day, and return to the hotel for a swim.

  3. Day one, evening

    Galle Face Green at sunset

    Arrive by 17:30, when the food carts open and families come out to walk beside the sea. Try isso wade (prawn fritters) and stay for the sunset over the Indian Ocean.

  4. Day two

    The square, the museum, the roof

    Independence Square in the morning and coffee at the Arcade beside it, then the National Museum. Give the afternoon to a massage or a swim, and finish on the rooftop at Cloud Café with the city lit up below you.

The full plan, with the practical notes on dress, money and traffic, is in our two-day guide. Not sure whether you need the second day? Here is how we would decide.

Where we eat

Hoppers at breakfast, crab at lunch.

Colombo has good food at every price, from street carts on the seafront to restaurants in restored colonial buildings. These are the places we recommend to friends and enjoy ourselves.

  • Galle Face Green, late in the afternoon: isso wade and kottu from the food carts beside the sea.
  • The Dutch Hospital in Fort: home to the well-known Ministry of Crab and several other good restaurants.
  • Arcade Independence Square: many restaurants under one roof, a good choice for groups.
  • A Sri Lankan breakfast: hoppers, string hoppers and kiribath with sambol. What is on the table.

At the hotel, both our restaurants are open to everyone: Amber Poolside during the day, from breakfast at 07:00 to high tea from 15:30 to 18:30, and Cloud Café on the roof from 17:30. More on eating in Colombo 3.

Art and culture

The Colombo that hangs on walls.

Some of Colombo's best art and architecture is a few minutes from the hotel. Geoffrey Bawa, the architect who shaped modern Sri Lankan design, had his office on Alfred House Road. It is now The Gallery Café, and the building makes it a good place for lunch.

  • Number 11, Bawa’s own house in Colombo 3, which can be visited by appointment.
  • The National Museum, where the throne and regalia of the Kandyan kings are kept.
  • The Lionel Wendt Art Centre, for exhibitions, theatre and music.
  • Gangaramaya Temple, whose rooms of gifts and curiosities are a museum in their own right.

At the hotel itself, the sculptures are made from scrap metal by the Sri Lankan artist Prageeth Manohansa. Our Story tells how it came to be here.

Where we shop

Handloom, ceramics and tea to take home.

Colombo is a good place to shop for things made in Sri Lanka.

  • Barefoot on Galle Road: colourful handloom cotton, with a gallery and a café behind the shop.
  • Paradise Road: well-made homeware, ceramics and gifts.
  • Laksala: Sri Lankan crafts at fixed prices, for presents without the haggling.
  • Pettah, in the morning: busy market streets selling spices, fabric and much more.

For tea, buy from a tea merchant in the city rather than at the airport. Ask for a single estate tea, look for the lion logo and ask for it to be vacuum packed. Our short guide to Ceylon tea explains the rest.

Colombo after dark

Colombo in the evening.

A good Colombo evening starts by the sea and ends on a rooftop. Galle Face Green is at its best at dusk, the Lotus Tower lights up across the city, and the evening air is cooler on a roof in Colombo 3.

  • Cloud Café & Bar, our rooftop restaurant and bar: open from 17:30 every evening, with last orders at 23:30.
  • The Loft, our indoor bar and lounge, keeps the same hours.

Sunday evenings are usually quiet. On Poya days, the monthly full moon holidays, no alcohol is served anywhere in Sri Lanka, so there is no happy hour and some restaurants close. To get back, take a metered tuk-tuk or use PickMe or Uber, or ask at the front desk and we will call a car for you.

Hidden Colombo

The city most visitors never see.

These are some of our favourite local experiences. Our front desk team is happy to suggest more, based on what you enjoy.

  • The coastal train south from Kollupitiya station, in second class with the windows open and the sea a few metres away.
  • Independence Square at six in the morning, when local people go running and walking before the heat.
  • Seema Malaka, early, before the visitors arrive and while Beira Lake is still.
  • A tailor: many Colombo tailors still make clothes to measure. Ask at the front desk for a recommendation.

Tell us at breakfast what kind of day you would like, and we will suggest where to go. Getting around Colombo covers the tuk-tuks, the apps and the train.

Before you head out.

The questions guests ask the front desk most about Colombo.

What are the best things to do near Colombo Court Hotel & Spa?

Independence Square and the Arcade beside it, Galle Face Green at sunset, Gangaramaya Temple and Seema Malaka on Beira Lake, and the National Museum are all a short tuk-tuk ride from the hotel on Alfred House Avenue in Colombo 3.

How many days do you need in Colombo?

Two days is enough to see the temples, Fort, Pettah, Independence Square and the museum at a relaxed pace, with an afternoon free. One night is best used to rest after a flight.

What should I wear to visit a temple in Colombo?

Cover your shoulders and knees, and take your shoes off before you go in. A light scarf or shawl is useful to carry.

What is a Poya day in Sri Lanka?

Poya is the monthly full moon holiday in Sri Lanka. No alcohol is sold or served on Poya days, including in hotel bars, and some restaurants close. Please check the date before planning an evening out.

What is the best way to get around Colombo?

Take a metered tuk-tuk, or use a ride app such as PickMe or Uber, which show the price before you book. The hotel can also arrange a car and driver, and airport transfers are available. Please contact the front office for rates.

When is the best time to visit Galle Face Green?

Late afternoon, from about half past five, when the food carts set up and the city comes out to walk beside the sea. Stay for the sunset.

Where should I buy Ceylon tea in Colombo?

From a tea merchant in the city rather than at the airport. Ask for a single estate tea, look for the Sri Lanka Tea Board lion logo, and ask for it to be vacuum packed.

Can I take the coastal train from Colombo?

Yes. Trains run south along the coast from Colombo Fort and Kollupitiya stations. Second class has windows that open, and tickets can be bought on the day.

Can I eat at Colombo Court without staying there?

Yes. Amber Poolside and Cloud Café are both open to everyone, not only hotel guests.
