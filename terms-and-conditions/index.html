01

Booking policy

A booking is confirmed once the advance payment set out in our payment policy has been received. When your booking is complete, we will send you a booking confirmation with the details of your booking. You must show your booking confirmation(s) at check-in, together with a valid photo identification document (national identity card, passport, driving licence or any other valid identification document). If you do not receive your booking confirmation(s) within twenty-four (24) hours of booking, please contact us at info@colombocourthotel.com or +94 11 464 5333.

Colombo Court Hotel & Spa was the first city hotel in South Asia to be certified CarbonNeutral®.

02

Check-in policy

Check-in time is 14.00 hrs on the day of arrival. Booked rooms will be held until 9.00 hrs on the day after the scheduled check-in date. After this time, the booking will be treated as a no-show, and Colombo Court Hotel & Spa shall be entitled to release the booked room(s) to other guests for the remaining dates of the stay. No refund will be given for a booking cancelled in this way.

Early check-in is subject to availability and is chargeable. To ensure your room is available for an early check-in, we recommend booking a night in advance.

03

Check-out policy

Check-out time is 12.00 noon on the day of departure. If you check out after this time, Colombo Court Hotel & Spa reserves the right to charge for one (1) additional night, or to move your belongings to the luggage storage facility (luggage storage charges may apply) and deny you further access to the room. Colombo Court Hotel & Spa shall not be liable for any loss of or damage to your belongings resulting from such action.

Late check-out is subject to availability and is chargeable.

04

Cancellation policy

  • For stays between 20 December and 31 March, bookings may be cancelled free of charge with at least 14 days’ written notice before the date of arrival.
  • For stays on all other dates, bookings may be cancelled free of charge with at least 24 hours’ written notice before the date of arrival.
  • Bookings cancelled with less notice than this, and no-shows, will be charged 100% of the full stay.
  • All no-shows will be charged 100% of the full stay.

Colombo Court Hotel & Spa reserves the right to cancel a confirmed booking because of an emergency, natural disaster or other unavoidable circumstance. In that case, Colombo Court Hotel & Spa will either refund the full amount paid by the customer or move the customer to another hotel of a similar standard.

05

Child policy

  • Children aged 5 years and under stay free of charge. They may share their parents’ bed, or an extra bed can be provided at USD 15 per night.
  • Children aged 6 to 11 years are charged 50% of the meal rate. An extra bed is required for this age group, at USD 15 per night.
  • Guests aged 12 years and over are charged the full adult rate.

Child rates include taxes and service charge. A complimentary baby cot is available for infants. Colombo Court Hotel & Spa does not offer a babysitting service.

06

Payment policy

  • An advance payment of 50% of the booking value is required to confirm a booking. The balance is payable on arrival.
  • Payments are not taken through this website. Our reservations team will send you the payment details with your booking confirmation.
  • For stays between 20 December and 31 March, the balance is due 14 days before the date of arrival.

We accept American Express, Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards. We accept payment in Sri Lankan rupees, US dollars, British pounds, Australian dollars and euros.

Colombo Court Hotel & Spa is entitled to request payment of any amounts outstanding from earlier check-outs or previous stays.

07

Damages

Colombo Court Hotel & Spa reserves the right to retain your payment details in order to compensate for any damage caused to the allocated room(s) or hotel premises by you or any persons in your party during your stay. Colombo Court Hotel & Spa further reserves the right to commence legal proceedings against you without notice.

08

Parking

Colombo Court Hotel & Spa provides free on-site car parking for guests. Spaces are limited. Cars are parked at the guest’s own risk, and the hotel cannot guarantee the security of any car or its contents. Please lock your car and keep your belongings out of sight. The hotel reserves the right to move vehicles when necessary to organise the parking area.

09

Airport transfers

Airport transfers are available on request. Please contact the front office for rates.

10

Pets

We’re unable to accommodate pets at the hotel. If you have any questions, please contact us before you book.

11

Smoking

Smoking is not permitted in any room at Colombo Court Hotel & Spa. If this policy is breached, Colombo Court Hotel & Spa has the right to charge a penalty equal to one room night at check-out.

12

Doctor on call

Colombo Court Hotel & Spa has a doctor on call twenty-four (24) hours a day. The hotel will bear the cost of the doctor’s call-out fee, and any further costs will be borne by the guest. Any serious injuries which cannot be treated by the hotel doctor will be referred to the nearest hospital for further treatment.
